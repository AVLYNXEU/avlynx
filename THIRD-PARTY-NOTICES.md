# Third-party notices

AVLYNX bundles one library and two fonts inside `index.html`.

| Component | Version | Use | Licence | Licence text |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| jsPDF | 4.2.1 | PDF export | MIT | `licenses/jsPDF-LICENSE.txt` |
| Atkinson Hyperlegible (Regular, Bold) | Google Fonts release | Text boxes on screen and in the PDF | SIL Open Font License 1.1 | `licenses/AtkinsonHyperlegible-OFL.txt` |
| Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono (Regular, Bold) | Google Fonts release | Text boxes on screen and in the PDF | SIL Open Font License 1.1 | `licenses/AtkinsonHyperlegibleMono-OFL.txt` |

jsPDF and Atkinson Hyperlegible are included unmodified. The Regular and Bold weights of Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono were made from the published variable font, because the PDF export needs fixed weights. Excel and CSV files are written and read by AVLYNX itself.
